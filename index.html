About NEXTEL

What is NEXTEL?

NEXTEL is a community hub where members get updates, announcements, and event flyers in one place, with a connected Telegram channel for real-time discussion.

Do I need Telegram to use this app?

You can browse updates and flyers directly in this app. Joining the linked Telegram channel is optional, for members who want live chat and notifications.
